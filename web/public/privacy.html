relaynet Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026.

This policy explains what relaynet, operated by Jonathan Beri, collects and why. Contact: privacy@relaynet.network.

What is collected

  • Account: your email address and an identifier from the sign-in provider (Auth0), when the account was created and last used, your tier and status.
  • API tokens: a one-way hash and short prefix of each token, its name, creation, last-use and expiry times. The token itself is never stored.
  • Usage: bytes sent and received and number of sessions, per month.
  • Connection records: for each connection your virtual machine opens, the destination address and port, protocol, whether it was allowed, bytes transferred and start and end time. These are kept for 30 days to investigate abuse reports, then deleted.
  • Network data: your IP address while a session is open, and in operational logs used to run and secure the service (kept 14 days).

relaynet does not record or inspect the contents of your traffic, and cannot read encrypted (TLS) traffic.

Why

To provide and secure the service, enforce limits and the Acceptable Use Policy, answer abuse reports, and contact you about your account. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Legal bases

Where data-protection law such as the GDPR applies: account, token and usage data are processed to provide the service you signed up for (contract); connection records and network data are processed to keep the service and the internet safe from abuse and to answer abuse reports (legitimate interests); emails about your account are part of the service.

Cookies and tracking

The dashboard stores your sign-in session in your browser (Auth0) and sets no advertising or analytics cookies. There are no third-party trackers.

Security

Traffic to relaynet is encrypted (TLS). API tokens are stored only as hashes. The operator panel is not reachable from the internet and requires multi-factor authentication. Database backups are kept in a private bucket.

Retention and deletion

Account, token and usage data are kept until you delete your account. You can do that from the dashboard; it removes them immediately. Connection records expire after 30 days, and database backups after 7 days. Records may be kept longer when needed for an open abuse investigation or a legal obligation.

Your rights

You can see your account data on the dashboard, and ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at privacy@relaynet.network. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, including complaining to a data protection authority.

Children

relaynet is not meant for anyone under 16, and I do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

Material changes will be announced on the site or by email before they apply.
