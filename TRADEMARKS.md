# Trademark policy

The code is open source (see `LICENSE`); the name is not. This policy says
how you can use the relaynet name, so people can tell the hosted service at
relaynet.network apart from copies, forks and other services built on the
same code. It aims to permit everything a fair-minded user, contributor or
reviewer would want to do.

The Apache License 2.0 (section 6) and the MIT License grant no rights in
names or marks; this policy does.

## What it covers

- the name **relaynet**, alone or in a longer name;
- the domain **relaynet.network**;
- any relaynet logo, once there is one.

These are unregistered marks of the project owner. Don't add ® to them.
"rootlessRelay" is the upstream project's name, not ours; this policy says
nothing about it.

## Fine without asking

- **Talking about relaynet**: articles, reviews, tutorials, videos,
  comparisons, talks and books, including critical ones, as long as you don't
  suggest we wrote or endorsed them.
- **Saying what your work does with it**: "works with relaynet",
  "a relaynet client for Go", "tested against relaynet.network".
- **Redistributing the unmodified software** under its own name, or with
  changes limited to packaging (a distribution package, a container image)
  that don't change its behaviour.
- **Running it yourself**, for yourself or inside your organization, and
  calling it relaynet internally.
- **Saying where a fork comes from**: "based on relaynet",
  "a fork of relaynet".
- **Non-commercial community use**: a meetup, a workshop, a study group, a
  conference talk title.

## Ask first

- **Naming a product, service, company or project** with "relaynet" in it,
  or something close enough to confuse (Relay-Net, RelaynetCloud,
  relaynet.io).
- **Offering a public or paid service** under the relaynet name. You're
  welcome to run the code as a service; give it your own name, and you may
  say it "runs relaynet's open-source software".
- **Calling a modified version relaynet.** Forks that change behaviour need
  their own name.
- **Domain names, app-store listings, social-media handles or package names**
  that contain "relaynet".
- **Merchandise**, and any use of a relaynet logo other than to link to the
  project.
- **Anything that suggests partnership, certification or endorsement.**

Write to support@relaynet.network; we answer, and we say yes to most
requests that don't confuse users about who runs what.

## Using the name well

- Write it in lowercase, as one word: **relaynet**.
- Don't combine it with your own mark into a new word, and don't alter any
  relaynet logo.
- If people could be confused, say plainly that you are not relaynet and
  that relaynet.network is the official service.

## If something's wrong

If you see a use that confuses people, or think we've got this policy wrong,
email support@relaynet.network. We'd rather sort it out by talking than by
anything formal.
